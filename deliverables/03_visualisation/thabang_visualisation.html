# CELL 4: BAR CHART - GDP Growth (South Africa)

# Use all years
df_all_years = df_total.copy()

# CELL 8: PIE CHART - Employment by Sector (South Africa)

latest_year = df_total['Year'].max()
df_sectors = df[df['TIME_PERIOD'] == latest_year].copy()
df_sectors = df_sectors[df_sectors['Sector'] != 'Sector: Total'].copy()
df_sectors['Sector_Clean'] = df_sectors['Sector'].str.replace('Sector: ', '')
df_sectors = df_sectors.sort_values('Employment_Thousands', ascending=False)

plt.figure(figsize=(10, 8))

# pie chart with labels outside to prevent overlap
wedges, texts, autotexts = plt.pie(
    df_sectors['Employment_Thousands'],
    labels=df_sectors['Sector_Clean'],
    autopct='%1.1f%%',
    startangle=90,
    pctdistance=0.85,  # Move percentages slightly outward
    labeldistance=1.1,  # Move labels further out
    colors=['#4472C4', '#70AD47', '#FFC000', '#ED7D31', '#A5A5A5', '#FF6B6B', '#9B59B6']
)

# percentage text bold and readable
for autotext in autotexts:
    autotext.set_fontsize(10)
    autotext.set_fontweight('bold')

# labels readable
for text in texts:
    text.set_fontsize(11)

plt.title(f'South Africa Employment by Sector ({latest_year})', fontsize=14, fontweight='bold')
plt.axis('equal')
plt.tight_layout()
plt.savefig('pie_sectors_sa.png', dpi=300)
plt.show()

print(" Pie chart saved as 'pie_sectors_sa.png'")

SOUTH AFRICA - VISUALIZATION SUMMARY¶

Charts Created:¶

1. Bar Chart - GDP Growth by Year¶

  • Shows South Africa's GDP growth rate for selected years
  • Easy to compare which years had highest/lowest growth
  • Y-axis starts at 0 for accurate comparison

2. Bar Chart - Employment by Sector¶

  • Shows which sectors employ the most people in South Africa
  • X-axis starts at 0 for accurate comparison

3. Line Chart - GDP Growth Over Time¶

  • Shows the trend of GDP growth over the years
  • ONE line moving from left to right
  • Helps identify patterns (increasing/decreasing)

4. Line Chart - Employment Over Time¶

  • Shows how employment has changed over the years
  • ONE line moving from left to right
  • Helps identify trends

5. Pie Chart - Employment by Sector¶

  • Shows the proportion of employment in each sector
  • All sectors add up to 100%
  • Easy to see which sector is largest/smallest

6. Scatter Plot - GDP vs Employment¶

  • Shows relationship between GDP growth and employment
  • Each point represents one year
  • Helps see if they are related

Key Findings:¶

  1. GDP growth was highest in 1964 at 8%
  2. GDP growth was lowest in 1992 at -2.14%
  3. Employment is highest in Other services sector
  4. Employment is lowest in Utilities sector
